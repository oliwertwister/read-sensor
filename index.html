About this image

This is a Meteosat Third Generation FCI product supplied by EUMETSAT EUMETView. The coordinate grid and Natural Earth Admin-0 country boundaries are fitted to the same requested WGS84 / CRS:84 extent as the satellite image; they are not inferred from image pixels.
